Key insights

High-bill risk: Customers charged $45+/day churn at 62% — 4× the average. Consider tiered pricing relief for heavy users.

International plan paradox: Customers with international plans churn at 35.9% vs 12.4% without — review international plan value proposition.

Service call threshold: Churn jumps to 64.7% at 5 service calls. Proactive outreach after call 3 could prevent churn.

Voicemail protects: Voicemail plan users churn at only 7.9% vs 16.7% — the plan builds stickiness. Promote it more aggressively.

Geographic hotspots: CA, MD, CO and SC account for disproportionate churn. Targeted regional retention campaigns warranted.
